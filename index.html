Privacy Policy — Arcsa Remote

Last updated: April 2026

Arcsa Remote is an unofficial third-party remote control app for Arcam SA10 and SA20 integrated amplifiers. This policy explains what data the app collects and how it is used.

Data We Collect

Arcsa Remote collects and stores only the following on your device:

  • The IP address of your amplifier (stored locally on your device only)

This information never leaves your device and is never transmitted to any server.

Data We Do Not Collect

  • No personal information
  • No usage analytics
  • No crash reports
  • No advertising identifiers
  • No location data

Network Usage

The app communicates directly with your Arcam amplifier over your local home network using Arcam's official IP control protocol (TCP port 50000). No data is sent to the internet.

Third-Party Services

Arcsa Remote does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

Children's Privacy

This app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any information from children.

Changes to This Policy

Any updates to this privacy policy will be reflected on this page with an updated date.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, you can open an issue on the app's GitHub repository.
