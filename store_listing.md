## Full Description

The official Arcam app doesn't work properly. This one does.

Arcsa Remote was built by an SA20 owner who got tired of the crashes and slow response times. It talks to your amplifier using Arcam's own IP control protocol — the same one custom installers use — so everything works exactly the way it should.

**Works over your home Wi-Fi. No cloud, no account, no Bluetooth, no subscription.**

---

Open the app and it finds your amp automatically. No IP addresses to type in, no setup screens to wade through. On subsequent launches it reconnects in under two seconds.

---

**What you can control**

Everything the front panel does, you can do from your phone. Volume with a smooth drag dial, mute, all eight inputs (Phono, AUX, PVR, AV, STB, CD, BD, SAT), balance, display brightness, and the auto-shutdown timer. Power on and standby work properly too — including the startup sequence delay the SA20 needs while its relay settles.

**DAC filter advisor**

The SA20 has seven DAC filters and most people never touch them because the names mean nothing. Arcsa has a two-question advisor — tell it what you listen to and whether you prefer detail or warmth — and it picks one for you and explains why. You can always override it manually.

**Diagnostics**

A dedicated screen pulls live data from the amp: heatsink and lifter temperatures, current sample rate (with a Hi-Res flag when you're above 88.2 kHz), DC offset and short-circuit status, which DAC filter is active, input signal presence, IP address, firmware version, and more.

---

**A few things worth knowing**

Your amp needs to be on your home network — Ethernet is more reliable than the SA20's built-in Wi-Fi. Your phone needs to be on the same network. The app uses port 50000, which is Arcam's standard control port and requires no router configuration.

This app only works with the SA10 and SA20. It will not work with other Arcam products.

No ads. No tracking. No data leaves your home network.
